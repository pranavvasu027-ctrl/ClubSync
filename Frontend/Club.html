About the Club
The Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC) of VIT Pune aims to produce successful entrepreneurs equipped with leadership qualities, using innovative and ethical business practices to make a global impact. EDC builds a community of like-minded, passionate entrepreneurs and gives every budding founder on campus a platform to learn, pitch, and grow.
"An idea without execution is just a dream."
